# Privacy Policy for DOIT!!!

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled in the **DOIT!!!** mobile application (hereafter referred to as the "App"), an aggressive reminder and to-do app. The App is designed to work locally on your device: your tasks and reminders are yours, and they stay on your phone.

---

### 1. Data Storage — Local Only
* **Your tasks live on your device:** All tasks, reminders, sounds and settings are stored in a local database on your phone. The App has **no accounts and no sign-up**, and the developer operates **no server** that receives your tasks.
* **No personal information:** The App does not collect, record or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses or location.
* **No analytics or tracking:** The App contains no analytics, profiling or tracking SDKs, and shows **no ads**.

### 2. Voice Features and Microphone
The App includes optional voice features (recording voice notes for reminders and spoken reminder announcements). Audio you record is stored locally on your device as part of your reminder. Speech is produced on-device using your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. Your audio is not uploaded to the developer.

### 3. Explanation of Required Permissions
The App requests only the permissions its reminder features need:

1. **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`** — to show reminder notifications.
2. **`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`** — so reminders fire at the exact time you set, even in power-saving modes.
3. **`USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT` / `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`** — to display the full-screen reminder alert over other apps and the lock screen. This is the core "won't leave you alone" feature, and it only ever shows your own reminders.
4. **`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`** — to restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
5. **`VIBRATE`** — for vibration patterns on reminder alerts.
6. **`RECORD_AUDIO`** — only for the optional voice-note feature, when you choose to record.
7. **`INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`** — included in the build; the App does not send your tasks, reminders or audio anywhere, and the developer runs no backend for the App.

### 4. Data Sharing
The App does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, because it does not collect data to share.

### 5. Children's Privacy
The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and is compliant with COPPA and GDPR data-minimization principles.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be applied directly to this document; users are advised to review it periodically.
